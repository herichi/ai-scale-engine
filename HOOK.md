# HOOK — Locked Hook Style

**Status: APPROVED / LOCKED (2026-07-28).**

## Pattern: Question hook

Opens with a direct question that puts the viewer's own stuck-ness or
unspoken thought right in front of them, before reframing and pointing to
the system. Used for short-form video hooks (10s scripts), especially
top-of-funnel content aimed at coaches who feel stuck at their current
income/pace of growth.

**Structure:** question → reframe/answer in one line → CTA.

## The 3 locked examples

1. *"Do you know the 3 mistakes keeping you stuck at the same income?"*
2. *"Did you ever ask yourself how to actually scale your business?"*
3. *"What if I told you that you could multiply your income without
   multiplying your hours?"*

## Full example script (10s target, ~30 words at 3.0 words/sec)

> "What if I told you that you could multiply your income without
> multiplying your hours? That's not a promise. That's the system. Join
> the AI Scale Engine — link in bio."

31 words → ~10.3s at 3.0 words/sec, ending on the locked CTA (see
`offer.md` Stage 4: *"Join the AI Scale Engine — link in bio. $29/month."*)
